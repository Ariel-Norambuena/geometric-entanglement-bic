\subsection{Robustness against control imperfections}
\label{subsec:protocol_parameter_robustness}

We also test the final Bell-state fidelity against independent control
imperfections in the complete two-stage protocol. The scan is performed with
the validated central-sideband effective model and reports the unconditional
fidelity \(F_{\Psi^+}(t_f)\) at the end of the sequence. The four maps in
Fig.~\ref{fig:protocol_parameter_robustness} correspond to errors in the local
loading pulse, Floquet-coupling calibration, passage time and CD strength, and
atomic detunings.

The protocol is highly tolerant to imperfect Floquet-coupling calibration:
over the full window
\(-0.10\le\delta u_{1,2}/u_{1,2}\le0.10\), the final fidelity remains above
\(0.995\). The loading pulse is also robust in the tested range, with
\(F_{\Psi^+}>0.971\) for area errors
\(|\delta A_\pi/A_\pi|\le0.10\) and phase errors
\(|\delta\varphi_\pi|\le0.1\pi\). The CD-assisted passage remains above
\(0.995\) for \(|\delta\alpha_{\rm CD}|\le0.025\) over the full scanned
duration window \(-0.5\le\delta T/T_0\le0.5\), and remains above \(0.99\) for
approximately \(-0.075\le\delta\alpha_{\rm CD}\le0.10\). The most restrictive
imperfection in this scan is the relative atomic detuning: for
\(|\Delta_{\rm r}|/\xi\le0.0125\), the final fidelity remains above \(0.99\)
for all tested common detunings \(|\Delta_{\rm c}|/\xi\le0.05\).
